01

Antenatal & Pregnancy Care

Pregnancy is one of life’s biggest journeys. We stay beside you through all of it — planning, checking, reassuring and preparing you for birth.

What we help with

  • Preconception counselling and planning
  • Confirmation of pregnancy and early-pregnancy care
  • Regular antenatal check-ups, scans and blood tests
  • Nutrition, supplements and safe exercise guidance
  • High-risk pregnancy monitoring
  • Birth planning and postnatal follow-up

Consider a visit if

  • You’re planning to conceive
  • You’ve had a positive pregnancy test
  • You have diabetes, thyroid or blood-pressure concerns
  • You’ve had a previous miscarriage or complicated pregnancy
02

Gynaecological Care

From routine check-ups to complex conditions — thorough assessment, clear explanations and, where surgery is advised, gentle minimal-access techniques.

What we help with

  • Routine women’s health check-ups and screening
  • Vaginal infections, discharge and pelvic pain
  • Fibroids, ovarian cysts and endometriosis
  • Contraception advice
  • Keyhole (laparoscopic) and other gynaecological surgery

Consider a visit if

  • You have pelvic pain or unusual discharge
  • You’ve noticed lumps, swelling or bleeding between periods
  • You’re due a routine check-up or screening
  • You’d like to discuss contraception
03

PCOS & Hormonal Disorders

Polycystic ovary syndrome and other hormonal conditions affect far more than periods. We look at the whole picture and plan care that fits your life and goals.

What we help with

  • PCOS diagnosis and long-term management
  • Irregular or absent periods
  • Acne, unwanted hair growth and hair thinning
  • Weight and metabolic concerns linked to hormones
  • Thyroid-related menstrual and fertility issues

Consider a visit if

  • Your cycles are consistently irregular
  • You have new acne or excess hair growth
  • You’re finding it hard to lose weight despite effort
  • You’re trying to conceive with irregular cycles
04

Menstrual Disorders

Periods that disrupt your life deserve attention. We find the cause, then treat it — so you can get on with your days.

What we help with

  • Heavy or prolonged periods
  • Painful periods (dysmenorrhoea)
  • Delayed, missed or irregular cycles
  • Premenstrual symptoms
  • Bleeding between periods or after intercourse

Consider a visit if

  • Pain keeps you from school, work or daily life
  • You soak through pads or tampons very quickly
  • Your periods have suddenly changed
  • You’ve missed periods and aren’t pregnant
05

Infertility Care

Finding it hard to conceive can feel isolating. Our approach is compassionate, methodical and honest — with a clear plan at each step and no pressure.

What we help with

  • Initial fertility assessment for you and your partner
  • Ovulation tracking and cycle optimisation
  • Investigation of tubal, uterine and hormonal factors
  • Treatment of underlying conditions such as PCOS, fibroids or endometriosis
  • Guidance on next steps, including referral where appropriate

Consider a visit if

  • You’ve been trying for a year (or six months if you’re over 35)
  • Your cycles are irregular
  • You’ve had recurrent pregnancy loss
  • You’d like advice before you start trying
06

Menopause Wellness

Menopause is a natural transition — but you shouldn’t have to struggle through it. We help you manage symptoms and protect your long-term health.

What we help with

  • Hot flushes, night sweats and sleep disturbance
  • Mood changes, low energy and brain fog
  • Vaginal dryness and urinary symptoms
  • Bone-health and heart-health guidance
  • Discussion of hormone and non-hormonal options

Consider a visit if

  • Your periods are becoming irregular in your 40s
  • Symptoms are affecting sleep, work or relationships
  • You’re bleeding after menopause
  • You’d like a wellness and screening review
