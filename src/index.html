When should I see a gynaecologist?

Whenever something feels different or worries you — painful or irregular periods, unusual discharge, pelvic pain, difficulty conceiving, or menopausal symptoms. Routine check-ups and screening are also worth doing regularly, even when you feel well. There is no “too small” a reason to ask.

How do I book an appointment?

Call 92652 59256 or 96544 47293, or send us a WhatsApp message from the contact page. We recommend booking ahead so that we can give you the time you deserve.

Is my consultation confidential?

Yes. Your health information is treated with complete discretion. You are welcome to come alone, with your partner, or with a family member — whatever you’re most comfortable with.

What should I bring to my first visit?

Any previous prescriptions, reports and scans; a list of medicines you currently take; and, if relevant, the date of your last period and a rough record of your cycles. If you’re pregnant, bring your earlier antenatal records.

Do you treat conditions that need surgery?

Dr. Ruchi is trained in minimal-access (keyhole) surgery. Surgery is only ever advised when it is truly the right option, and every choice, benefit and risk will be explained before you decide.

What if I have an emergency?

Heavy bleeding, severe abdominal pain, fainting, leaking of fluid, or reduced baby movements in pregnancy need urgent attention — please go to the nearest hospital emergency department straight away.
