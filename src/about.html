Dr. Ruchi

MBBS · DGO · DNB · MRCOG · FMAS · DMAS

Dr. Ruchi is an Obstetrician & Gynaecologist practising at Her Space Women’s Clinic in Malviya Nagar, New Delhi. Her qualifications span the full breadth of the specialty — from the National Board diploma and Royal College membership that anchor her practice in obstetrics and gynaecology, to fellowship and diploma training in minimal-access surgery.

Patients come to her for pregnancy care, menstrual and hormonal concerns, fertility guidance, everyday gynaecological health and menopause — and find a doctor who takes the time to listen, explains things without jargon, and involves them in every decision.

Qualifications, explained

What the letters mean for you.

MBBS

Bachelor of Medicine & Bachelor of Surgery

The foundation of medical training — broad clinical knowledge across every branch of medicine.

DGO

Diploma in Obstetrics & Gynaecology

Focused postgraduate training in pregnancy, childbirth and women’s reproductive health.

DNB

Diplomate of the National Board

A nationally recognised postgraduate specialist qualification, reflecting in-depth training and examination.

MRCOG

Member, Royal College of Obstetricians & Gynaecologists (UK)

An internationally respected benchmark of knowledge and clinical skill in obstetrics and gynaecology.

FMAS

Fellowship in Minimal Access Surgery

Advanced training in keyhole (laparoscopic) techniques — smaller incisions, less pain and faster recovery where surgery is needed.

DMAS

Diploma in Minimal Access Surgery

Formal training in minimal-access surgical methods that complements her fellowship.
